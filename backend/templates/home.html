About the Project

What is SignLang?

SignLang is a Final Year Group Project developed to bridge the communication gap for people who are deaf or hard of hearing. Using computer vision and machine learning, our system captures hand gestures through a camera and converts them to text in real-time — supporting both American Sign Language (ASL) and Indian Sign Language (ISL).

Vision

Provide a free, accessible tool for the deaf and hard-of-hearing community to communicate effortlessly with anyone.

Mission

Leverage AI, computer vision, and NLP to make sign language interpretation fast, accurate, and available to everyone.

Impact

Over 70 million deaf people worldwide and 18 million in India alone can benefit from tools that bridge sign language and text.

Process

How It Works

A three-step pipeline converts sign language gestures into readable text output.

01

Capture

The system uses your device camera to capture live hand gestures frame by frame in real time.

02

Process

MediaPipe landmarks extract 21 hand joint points per frame, which are fed into our trained AI classification model.

03

Output

Predicted signs are assembled into words and sentences using NLP, displayed instantly as readable text on screen.

Features

Key Features

Everything you need for seamless sign language recognition and communication.

Real-time Detection

Gestures are processed and converted to text with minimal latency using frame-by-frame AI analysis.

High Accuracy

Our trained neural network model achieves over 98% accuracy on both ASL and ISL gesture recognition tasks.

Two Sign Languages

Supports American Sign Language (ASL) and Indian Sign Language (ISL) with language-specific trained models.

Accessible UI

Designed with inclusivity in mind — large readable text, high contrast support, and clear navigation.

Text-to-Speech

Translated text can be read aloud using built-in speech synthesis for two-way accessible communication.

Secure & Private

All processing is done locally on your device. No video is uploaded to any server — your privacy is protected.
